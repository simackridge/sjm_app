Fair Usage Policy

Please read this policy alongside your service plan terms and conditions.

1. Purpose of this policy

This Fair Usage Policy is designed to keep our service plans practical, sustainable and fair for all customers. It explains the type of usage considered reasonable under your cover.

2. General principle

Your service plan is intended for normal domestic residential use and routine support relating to the covered heating system. It is not intended to cover excessive, repeated or avoidable callouts caused by misuse, neglect, vandalism, poor system conditions, or failure to follow previous advice or repair recommendations.

3. Reasonable use includes

  • Routine annual servicing included within your selected plan
  • Normal domestic breakdown attendance where covered under the plan
  • Reasonable contact regarding system performance, faults or service scheduling

4. Examples of unfair or excessive use

  • Repeated callouts for the same issue where recommended remedial works have been declined
  • Problems caused by sludge, scale, poor water quality, frozen condensate, low system pressure not due to covered faults, or lack of maintenance outside the scope of the plan
  • Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, third parties, builders, decorators, or unauthorised repairs
  • Commercial, business, or non-domestic use of equipment covered by a domestic plan
  • Access issues, unsafe working conditions, or appointments missed repeatedly without notice

5. System condition

Cover is based on the system being in a reasonable and serviceable condition at the point cover begins. Where major pre-existing faults, unsafe conditions, installation defects, or non-compliant work are discovered, we may recommend further repair, correction, or system improvements before full ongoing cover can continue.

6. Parts, repairs and limitations

Any work carried out remains subject to inspection, diagnosis, access, parts availability and economic viability. Where a system or component is obsolete, unsafe, beyond practical repair, or repeatedly unreliable, we may recommend replacement instead of continued repair.

7. Missed appointments and access

Customers must provide safe and reasonable access to the property and heating appliance. Repeated missed appointments or inability to gain access may result in delays, extra charges, or suspension of service benefits.

8. Review of usage

If we believe usage under a plan has become excessive or falls outside reasonable domestic cover expectations, we reserve the right to review the account, discuss the issue with the customer, and apply reasonable restrictions, recommendations or changes in line with the service plan terms.
